# Game Over Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** His own mistakes and being too slow kill him, on the road and in the flat. Each death plays a short scene
with her, then GAME OVER, then he retries that mission or flat act.

**Architecture:** The headless cores decide every death:
- `RoadDirector.die()` on the road;
- `HorrorRuntime.die()` in the flat, fed by `StoryDirector`, `EntityController` and `VisitorEvent` through `killed`
  signals or run results.

Each emits one new signal, `EventBus.player_died(cause)`. The scenes (`RoadMain`, `Main`) answer it: she comes for
the camera, then the HUD shows a GAME OVER card. Its "Try again" asks `Journey` to reload the scene at the mission or
act snapshot, the way `--mission=N` and `debug_skip` already do.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7.2, typed GDScript, the repo's own test runner (`tools/test.sh`). Voices come from
`~/condo-voice/gen.py` (Dia + Qwen3-ASR, outside the repo).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-game-over-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Godot 4.7.2 at `~/tools/godot-4.7.2/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot`; typed GDScript; Compatibility renderer; web and desktop.
- Test command: `GODOT=~/tools/godot-4.7.2/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot tools/test.sh --filter=<substring>`; the full suite without `--filter` (287 tests before this plan).
- The core (`RoadDirector`, `StoryDirector`, `HorrorRuntime`) decides every death and its timing; the presentation never does. The bots play it headless.
- No shader compiles mid-game: anything newly drawn in the flat is drawn by `Main._compile_shaders` first.
- Web pck stays under 9936 KiB (`tools/smoke.sh`; 9447 KiB today).
- Summed peak at the master ≤ −6.1 dBFS (`docs/architecture.md`, "Audio levels").
- Changing a voiced line's text drops its voice until it is made again (`tests/unit/test_voices.gd`).
- Never run `tools/export.sh` in `~/the-condo`, only in this worktree. Commit on `feat/game-over`; never merge or push without the owner's OK.
- Commit messages end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **A death while a choice or caption is up:** the choice panel closes and no caption is left over the GAME OVER
   card. Task 6 tests this.
2. **A rule broken after the ending is decided:** for example the caught ending's lines, or Mei's look after the
   30 m decide point. It never kills, and `die()` refuses once `ending` is set or the walk is decided. Tasks 1 and 5
   test this.
3. **Retrying twice in a row:** the count is the mission start's both times, not growing with each failed try, and
   no EventBus connection is left behind. Task 6 tests this.
4. **The mouse is captured when he dies** (desktop and web): "Try again" must be clickable. `show_game_over()` makes
   the mouse visible. Task 6 tests this.
5. **A flat retry after each road ending:** `Journey.road_ending` survives the reload, so the flat's road-dependent
   lines and endings still match. Task 7 tests this.
